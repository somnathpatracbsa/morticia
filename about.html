About Morticia

Morticia was a black Indian Labrador — the kind of dog who didn't wait to be invited into your lap. She made herself at home from the very first hour, and she never stopped.

She came to us at around 10 years old, already a full personality. She had her routines, her preferences, her disapproving look when dinner was late. She had opinions. She had grace.

She loved food with a sincerity that was almost philosophical. Car rides made her ears fly and her eyes go wide. And cuddles — she gave them as much as she asked for them, with her whole heavy warm body.
