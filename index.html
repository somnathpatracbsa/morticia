Who she was

A soul too vivid to forget

Morticia came into our lives on 2 June 2021 — a 10-year-old black indie lab with a lifetime of stories already behind her, and somehow, all the energy and love in the world still ahead. She chose us as much as we chose her.

For nearly five years she was the first face we saw in the morning and the warm weight beside us at night. She had opinions about mealtimes, strong feelings about car windows, and a gift for finding exactly the right person to lean against.

This place is for her. Built slowly, with love, one memory at a time.
