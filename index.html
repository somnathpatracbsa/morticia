Who she was

A soul too vivid to forget

Morticia came to us on 2 June 2021 — a black indie lab of around 10 years, already a full personality. She had her routines, her opinions, and a gift for finding exactly the right person to lean against.

For nearly five years she was the first face we saw in the morning and the warm weight beside us at night. She made every single one of them better.
